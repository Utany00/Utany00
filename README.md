# Hi, I am Uta 👋

Product leader. I help companies redesign how they work so AI becomes part of the operating model, not a feature bolted onto the side.

I was CPO at iHeartMedia, and before that I built and led product experience teams at JPMorgan Chase, Nickelodeon, Outbrain and WW International. Somehow, I am also a named inventor on nine US patents (fun days). These days I co-founded **ZeroFHE**, which works in the fully homomorphic encryption space, and I advise leadership teams in the US and across the DACH region.

## What I work on

- **AI-native operating models.** Redesigning workflows so people and agents share the work sensibly, and the org can actually absorb the speed.
- **Decision velocity.** Why good teams stall, and how to make fewer, better, faster calls.
- **Coherence over output.** AI made building cheap. Incoherence is now the expensive part.
